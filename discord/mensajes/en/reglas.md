# 📜 Community rules
We want a healthy place for every production company. Many players are minors, so let's look out for each other.

**1. Respect first.** No insults, harassment, threats, racism, sexism, homophobia, or mocking someone's country, accent or skill.
**2. Family-friendly.** No sexual, violent, drug or gambling content. That includes names, avatars and statuses.
**3. No spam or ads.** No chain messages, repeated messages, mass mentions or invites to other servers, groups or channels.
**4. Zero real money.** No buying, selling or trading credits, gear or accounts for money, PayPal, top-ups or gifts. Accounts can only change owners through the game's **official transfer**. Credits never turn into money.
**5. Your account is yours.** Never share your password, codes or card details. Staff will **never** ask for them. Anyone who DMs you claiming to be staff or offering free credits is a scammer: report them.
**6. No personal data.** Don't post phone numbers, emails, addresses or photos of other people (or yourself).
**7. Play fair.** No cheats, hacks, modded APKs or bug abuse. Found a bug? Report it in {#errores}.
**8. Right place, right language.** Use the right channel and that channel's language. Support in {#soporte}, bugs in {#errores}, ideas in {#sugerencias}.
**9. No impersonation.** Not of staff, QF Producciones or content creators.
**10. Discord rules apply.** Follow the [Discord Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines) and your country's minimum age.
---
# ⚖️ Sanctions
Depending on severity, staff may: delete the message · warn · mute for 1 hour → 24 hours → 7 days · ban.
**Instant ban:** scams, real-money trading, asking for passwords, sexual content, sharing other people's personal data, serious threats or alt accounts to dodge a sanction.

Discord and in-game chat sanctions are separate, but scams are also reported to the game.

**Think it was a mistake?** Contact support from inside the game or by email: {CORREO}. Please don't argue about sanctions in public channels.

**Official staff:** only members with the {@&admin}, {@&mod} or {@&soporte} role. If in doubt, ask in {#soporte}.
