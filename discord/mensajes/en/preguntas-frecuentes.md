# ❓ FAQ
**Where do I get help with my account or a payment?**
Only **inside the game** (open **Support**, pick the purchase and explain what happened) or by email: {CORREO}. That way we can check your purchase safely. In {#soporte} the community can help with general questions.

**I paid but didn't get my item. What now?**
Open a case in the game and select that purchase. Never post screenshots with card details or your email on Discord.

**Can staff ask for my password?**
**Never.** Not your password, codes or card details. We also never DM you first. Real staff have the {@&admin}, {@&mod} or {@&soporte} role.

**Can I sell my account, credits or gear?**
For real money, **no** (it's the most common scam). Gear is sold **only on the in-game market**, for credits. Accounts can only change owners through the game's **official transfer**, for a fixed fee.

**How do I get my roles?**
Link your account in {#vincular} with `/vincular` and the code the game gives you. You get {@&verificado}, your server and, if it applies, {@&vip}, {@&comprador} or {@&presidente}.

**What are game servers?**
Each server holds about 1,000 production companies and is named after a city. New players join the newest one so everyone starts on equal footing. Open servers:
{SERVIDORES}

# 🏆 Associations and events
**What is an association?**
Your team of up to 20 production companies, with its own chat. Creating one costs $5,000 in-game money (free with VIP). Find or recruit in {#reclutamiento}.

**Which online events are there?**
• **Weekly Production League:** Monday to Sunday, association jobs.
• **Mega Festival:** your association produces a giant festival together.
• **Daily Tech Challenge:** a new challenge every day.
• **Production Duel:** challenge another company for the same contract.
• **International Cups:** your server against other servers.
• **International Networks Challenge:** beat the big TV networks.
Dates and times in {#eventos-en-curso} (Ecuador time, UTC-5).

**How does the market work?**
Inside the game, with credits: you can list gear if you have bought credits at least once, and a small fee applies. {#mercado} is for **talking** about prices only.

**I found a bug / I have an idea.**
Bugs in {#errores} (use the template), ideas in {#sugerencias}. Search first and react instead of reposting.

**Do you make content about the game?**
We love it! Ask for the {@&creador} role in {#soporte} with a link to your channel.
