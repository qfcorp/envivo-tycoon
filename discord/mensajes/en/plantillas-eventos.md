# Event templates (copy and paste)

**Where:** 🎪┃eventos-en-curso (events), 🆕┃novedades-del-juego (updates), 🟢┃estado-del-servidor (maintenance). Press **Copy**, paste in Discord, replace the [brackets], then **Publish**.
Times: https://hammertime.cyou → `<t:...:F>` (everyone sees their own time) and `<t:...:R>` ("in 2 hours"). Ping `@🔔 Avisos de eventos` only when an event **starts**.

## Weekly Production League (Monday)
```
@🔔 Avisos de eventos
# 🏆 The Weekly Production League has started!
From <t:[UNIX_MONDAY]:F> to <t:[UNIX_SUNDAY]:F>.
Your association has **12 jobs** this week: [e.g. cover 3 weddings, stream 5 events without drops, win 2 duels]. Every job adds points for your association.
📈 Leagues: Neighborhood → Provincial → National → International → World (top teams go up, bottom teams go down).
🎁 Rewards: league tokens for the reward shop and rare gear in the top leagues.
No association yet? Find one in #reclutamiento-de-asociaciones. 🎥
```

🎁 Rewards for your contribution and the festival's total quality: goals at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%.
