## Nombre de la app

EN VIVO Tycoon

## Descripción breve

Run your event production company: cameras and live streaming in a tycoon

## Descripción completa

Can you picture yourself broadcasting the championship final? You start in a garage with the old camera of Don Ramiro, your mentor, and a dream: turn your tiny production company into the best in the country.

EN VIVO ("LIVE" in Spanish) Tycoon is a management game about the real world of live events and broadcasting, made in Ecuador by people who do this job every day.

★ FROM GARAGE TO STADIUM
Cover church services, weddings, press conferences, local football matches, graduations, brand launches, concerts and pro football games. Each event needs different cameras, audio, internet and crew. Grow from a garage to an office, a studio and a full production center.

★ LIVE INCIDENTS
Anything can happen mid-broadcast: a cable breaks, the internet drops, it rains, the power goes out, the camera operator is late, the signal freezes. You decide how to fix it in seconds. Your choices shape the quality, the client's stars and your fame.

★ YOUR CREW
Hire camera operators, directors, sound engineers and streaming technicians. They level up with every event, but they also get tired, ask for raises and may quit if you neglect them.

★ BROADCAST GEAR
Cameras, switchers, audio mixers, wireless mics, encoders, backup routers, UPS units and more, inspired by real equipment but with made-up brands. Gear wears out with use: repair it in time or it will fail at the worst moment.

★ A STORY WITH A RIVAL
Six chapters with their own characters. MegaVisión, the rival company, will fight you for the best contracts. To win the duel you have to beat their quality.

★ PLAY WITH OTHER COMPANIES
• Player market to buy and sell gear with credits (opens after your first purchase; credits can't be exchanged for real money).
• Alliances of up to 20 companies and a moderated chat with report and block buttons.
• Online events: Weekly League, Daily Challenge, Mega Festival and Production Duel.

★ FREE TO PLAY
The game is free to download and the whole story can be played without paying. It offers optional in-app purchases: credit packs, one-time offers (welcome, starter pack and achievement offers), temporary boosts, a Monthly Card and time-limited passes (such as the 30-day VIP Pass). Every item shows its contents before you buy and nothing renews automatically. No ads.

★ WORKS OFFLINE
Story mode works without a connection and your game saves automatically. Create an account if you want to save to the cloud, make purchases or play with other companies.

Support inside the game (Help & support) and by email.
