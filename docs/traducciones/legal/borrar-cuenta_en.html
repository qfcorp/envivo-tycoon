Delete your account

Last updated: 23 October 2026

You can delete your EN VIVO Tycoon account whenever you want, from the game or without opening the game, by email. It is free.

Option 1: from the game (instant)

  1. Open the game (at juego.tukanec.com or in the Android app) and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Account → Delete my account.
  3. Type your password and the word BORRAR to confirm. The account is deleted at that moment.

Option 2: without the app, by email

If you do not have the app, cannot sign in or no longer remember your password:

  1. Write to soporte@tukanec.com from the email you used to create your account, with the subject "Borrar mi cuenta" (delete my account).
  2. Tell us the account's email and, if you remember it, your company's name.
  3. To protect your account, we may send a code or a question to that email to confirm that you are the owner.
  4. We delete the account and confirm it by email within 15 days at most (usually much sooner).

Write the email to delete my account

If you no longer have access to that email, write to us from another one and we will ask for other details to confirm that the account is yours (for example, the number of a purchase). You can also request it from the "Help & support" option in the game.

What is deleted

  • Your email, your name, your company's name, your alias and your password.
  • Your game saved in the cloud, your credits, your VIP and your Monthly Card, your equipment and anything you had for sale on the market.
  • Your chat messages, your blocks and reports, your support tickets and your place in your alliance.
  • The link with your Discord account, if you had one.

What is kept and for how long

  • Your purchase data (date, product, amount and billing details) is kept for 7 years, as required by Ecuador's tax rules (SRI).
  • A security record that the account was deleted, without your IP address.
  • Encrypted backups rotate automatically: deleted data disappears from them within 6 months at most.

We never store your card details.

Before deleting

  • Deleting the account cannot be undone and what you bought is lost.
  • If you want a refund for a purchase you did not use, request it before deleting the account (see the terms, section 12).
  • A game saved only on your device (without an account) is deleted by clearing the browser data or uninstalling the app.
