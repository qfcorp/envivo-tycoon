Privacy policy

Last updated: [FECHA]

1. Data controller

QFCORP SOCIEDAD POR ACCIONES SIMPLIFICADA (QF PRODUCCIONES), RUC (Ecuadorian tax ID) [RUC], located at [DIRECCIÓN], Ecuador. Contact: [CORREO DE SOPORTE] or "Help & support" inside the game. We process your data in accordance with Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales).

2. What data we keep

  • Your account: email, name and your password (stored encrypted so that nobody, not even us, can read it).
  • Your game: the game progress you save in the cloud, your credits, what you have received and your VIP Pass.
  • Your purchases: product, amount, date, and the cardholder details you enter when paying (names, ID number, email and mobile number), which we need to issue the electronic invoice (SRI).
  • Community: your company's public name, your alliance, chat messages (deleted after 30 days), reports and blocks, and your listings and sales on the market.
  • Support: the tickets you send us from "Help & support" and a small technical diagnostic sent by the game (version, language, screen size, game day), without your card details or your password.
  • Security: the IP address and browser you connect with, to protect your account and prevent fraud (for example, limiting password attempts).

3. Your card details: we do NOT store them

Payment is processed by Pago Plux. Your card details (number, expiration date and CVV) are encrypted in your browser and sent encrypted to Pago Plux. We do not see them, do not store them and do not write them into any log. We only receive from Plux the payment result and its transaction number.

4. What we use them for

  • Giving you access to your account and saving your game.
  • Processing and delivering your purchases, and issuing your invoice.
  • Providing support and answering your messages.
  • Protecting the game against cheating and fraud.

We do not sell your data. We only share it with Pago Plux (for payment), with the SRI (for the invoice) and when a competent authority requests it in accordance with the law.

5. Where and for how long

Data is stored on our server. The session cookie lasts 60 days and only serves so that you don't have to type your password every time. Purchase data is kept for as long as Ecuador's tax rules require; everything else, for as long as your account exists.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, or to delete your account, and you can also object to its use or request its portability. Write to us from your account email to [CORREO DE SOPORTE]. When your account is deleted, your game, your credits and your purchases are lost. We will only keep the billing data that the law requires us to keep.

7. Minors

If you are a minor, use the game with permission from your parents or guardians. They can ask us to access or delete your data.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will let you know in the game.
