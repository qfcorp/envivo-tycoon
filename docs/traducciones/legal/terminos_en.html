Delivery is immediate and automatic to your game account as soon as your bank approves the payment. To receive your purchase you must be logged in to your account. If you don't see your purchase after a few minutes, write to us with the purchase number and we will help you.

6. VIP Pass

The VIP Pass lasts 30 days from the purchase (if you already had an active VIP, 30 days are added at the end). It does not renew automatically and you are not charged again without your permission. The daily bonus can be claimed once per day (Ecuador time).

7. One-time offers, Monthly Card and boosts

The welcome offer and the starter pack can be bought only once per account.

The Monthly Card lasts 30 days: each day (Ecuador time) you can claim 20 credits inside the game. Days you don't claim do not accumulate. It does not renew automatically; if you buy another one while it is active, 30 days are added at the end.

Boosts are temporary help (crew or equipment for a few events, event insurance or removing your team's fatigue). They are used inside your game and, once used, are not returned.

The new owner confirms it with their own email and a code. When it is completed, all of the previous owner's sessions are closed, their market listings are withdrawn and the account cannot be transferred again or sold on the market for 7 days. Selling or sharing accounts outside this option is not allowed and we do not provide support in those cases.
